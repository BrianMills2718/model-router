1.Goal: what this plan is for and why

Outcome. Brian's subagent routing sends some work to Haiku, sets the reasoning level by tier, applies across sessions, rests on research and first principles, and has a feedback system that improves the rules over time.

North star

Subagent work runs on the cheapest model and effort that is safe, and the rules improve from real outcomes.

Thesis

Effort is the cheaper lever than a second model; Haiku is safe only where a cheap check exists; outcomes show loud failures but not silent ones.

What would prove it wrong

If simple-tier runs show a bad-outcome rate above the weekly review's raise threshold (12%), the Haiku tier is too wide.

Not for

Changing the main conversation's model, which a plugin cannot do.
